PAPER 01 2014

A matrix equation
on triangulated Riemann surfaces

Proc. Japan Acad. Ser. A Math. Sci. 90(2): 37-42
DOI: 10.3792/pjaa.90.37

In this paper, we define a matrix and call this matrix the associate matrix. Then, we prove that among the three matrices, which are a period matrix, a combinatorial period matrix which is introduced by Wilson and an associate matrix, there exists a matrix equation. $$ \Pi = \overline{\Pi_K} - \overline{\Lambda_K} $$

PAPER 02 2016

The asymptotic behavior of holomorphic 1-cochains

Kodai Mathematical Journal 39(2): 297-317
DOI: 10.2996/kmj/1467830139

A holomorphic 1-form is characterized by its periods. So is a holomorphic 1-cochain. We consider relations between a holomorphic 1-form and a holomorphic 1-cochain which have the same periods and show that holomorphic 1-cochains provide an approximation of holomorphic 1-forms $$ \iota_{\omega}(a_j) = \int_{a_j} \omega $$

holomorphic 1-forms holomorphic 1-cochains

Stochastic Data-Driven Differential Equations

Zenode / Report
DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.22670671

In this report, we construct a proposal model, a surrogate time-series AI model that combines stochastic differential equations with machine learning. By incorporating the structure of stochastic dynamics as an inductive bias, we aim to improve the robustness and trustworthiness of the AI model. $$ dS_t^{AI} = S_t^{AI} \bigg(\frac{\mu_T^{AI}(F)}{T}dt + \frac{\sigma_T^{AI}(F)}{\sqrt{T}}dW_t\bigg) $$

TECHNICAL REPORT 02 2026

Data-Driven Probability Measures

Zenode / Report
DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.22670565

In general, binary classification provides class predictions rather than the true underlying probabilities. In this report, we construct a surrogate AI model which estimates plausible probabilities from data using the Central Limit Theorem. $$ \mathbb{P}^{AI}(X=1|F,K) = \frac{\sum_{j=1}^{K}j\cdot\mathbb{P}\bigg(\overline{Z}^K(F)=\frac{j}{K}\bigg)}{K\cdot\sum_{j=1}^K\mathbb{P}\bigg(\overline{Z}^K(F)=\frac{j}{K}\bigg)} $$

Python PyTorch Probability
PAPER 03 2026

Rayleigh quotients of holomorphic cochains

Zenode / Preprint
DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.22638782

Using Rayleigh quotients associated with the combinatorial Hodge star operator, we show that the Rayleigh quotients of holomorphic 1-cochains converge to 1 at a linear rate as the mesh size tends to zero. As applications, we obtain convergence rate estimates for holomorphic 1-cochains and combinatorial period matrices. $$ \rho_{i\bigstar}(\sigma_j) = \frac{\langle i \bigstar \sigma_j, \sigma_j\rangle_C}{\langle \sigma_j, \sigma_j\rangle_C} $$

holomorphic 1-cochains Rayleigh quotients combinatorial period matrices
